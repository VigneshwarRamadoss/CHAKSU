# CHAKSU UI/UX Design Brief

## 1. The design assignment

Create a premium, award-caliber B2C streetwear storefront that converts CHAKSU's strongest asset—the six-letter word shape—into a complete product, content, and commerce system.

The experience should feel cinematic at entry, editorial while exploring, and exceptionally clear at the point of decision.

### Design tension

**80% disciplined system + 20% controlled damage.**

Discipline supplies trust: grid, product truth, readable type, consistent states, and restrained color. Controlled damage supplies memory: crop, scale, xerox texture, abrupt surface changes, and the K-derived device.

## 2. Creative north star

### Desired feeling

> You have entered a precise underground publication that also happens to be the easiest premium streetwear store to trust.

### Three design principles

1. **Desire first, clarity immediately after.** The opening image can withhold detail for a moment; the interface may not withhold the offer.
2. **Proof is part of the art direction.** Fabric macro, construction, fit, movement, and service should look as intentional as campaign photography.
3. **CHAKSU owns the crop.** Oversized wordmark fragments and the central K create recognition; generic grunge and borrowed symbols do not.

## 3. Competitive anti-patterns to avoid

| Source lesson | Avoid | CHAKSU response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VYBN strategy | Cinematic product page with an implicit proposition; story and shop disconnected. | One promise above the fold; every story links to proof and products. |
| Bonkers Corner | Thousands of choices, repeated sale cues, return ambiguity, choice overload. | Edited range, intent-led curation, truthful promotions, one policy source. |
| Bluorng | Desire is stronger than first-time clarity; low-contrast navigation and policy contradictions. | Strong contrast, visible value proof, concise service summary on PDP/cart. |
| Urban Monkey | Assortment sprawl, sale prominence, inconsistent delivery language. | One hero franchise, controlled depth, one operational promise. |
| Almost Gods reference | Tiny commerce text, extreme sparseness, giant media payload, visual language too close to a reference brand. | 16 px body baseline, performance budgets, original CHAKSU palette/type/campaign. |

## 4. Brand expression

### Positioning expression

- **Functional:** Statement silhouettes with fit, material, make, and service proof.
- **Emotional:** Earned confidence.
- **Social:** Recognised by people who notice construction and culture, not only logos.
- **Cultural:** Contemporary South-Indian movement and after-dark city life, expressed through real creators and place—not token motifs.

### Voice

- Short, exact, confident.
- Product copy names facts before adjectives.
- Editorial copy may be evocative but stays concrete.
- No fake insider slang, vague “for the culture,” “luxury” without proof, or all-caps walls.

### Copy examples

| Role | Good | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | `Statement silhouettes. Proof in every detail.` | `Redefining luxury streetwear for the culture.` |
| Product | `220 GSM compact jersey. Relaxed shoulder. Built for warm nights.` | `Unparalleled premium quality.` |
| Scarcity | `24 units remain in this color.` only if live inventory supports it | `Hurry! Selling fast!` |
| Service | `Exchange eligible for 14 days. See exact conditions.` | `Easy returns` when exclusions apply |

## 5. Visual system

### Color palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0A0A0A` | Primary dark surface and text |
| Bone | `#F2EFE6` | Primary light surface |
| Paper | `#FAF9F5` | Commerce reading surface |
| Concrete | `#77736B` | Secondary text and structure; verify contrast |
| Deep indigo | `#0B1836` | Campaign night field |
| Sodium | `#FF8A1F` | Warm campaign highlight; not default CTA |
| Acid | `#D7FF26` | Rare action/release marker, maximum ~5% of a viewport |
| Error | `#C8342D` | Validation/error with icon/text |
| Success | `#166B4A` | Success with icon/text |
| Focus | `#3B6FFF` | High-visibility focus ring on light/dark contexts |

Black/bone is the structural system. Indigo/sodium belongs primarily to photography and campaign surfaces. Acid is a navigational or release beat, never a decoration applied everywhere.

### Contrast rules

- Body text: at least 4.5:1.
- Large display text: at least 3:1, but aim higher over photography.
- Use fixed scrims behind hero copy; do not rely on the image remaining dark.
- Concrete text may require a darker token on Paper/Bone.

### Grid

| Viewport | Columns | Outer gutter | Column gap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 320-599 | 4 | 16 px | 12 px |
| 600-899 | 8 | 24 px | 16 px |
| 900-1439 | 12 | 32-48 px | 20 px |
| 1440+ | 12 | max 64 px | 24 px |

Reading content max width: 720 px. Commerce canvas may extend to 1600 px; hero remains full bleed. Product descriptions never span the full canvas.

### Spacing scale

`4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 192` px.

- Component interiors: 12-24 px.
- Commerce section gaps: 64-96 px desktop, 48-64 px mobile.
- Editorial scene gaps: 96-192 px desktop where narrative requires it.
- Never use emptiness to compensate for weak hierarchy.

### Shape

- Default radius: 0 px.
- Inputs/operational controls: 2-4 px only if needed for legibility.
- Pills reserved for filters/status, not general decoration.
- Borders: 1 px; focus: 2 px plus offset.
- Shadows are rare and functional: menu/cart elevation only.

### Texture

Allowed: xerox grain, halftone, sticker residue, registration shift, ink bleed, marker annotation.

Rules:

- Apply as campaign assets or pseudo-element overlays at low opacity.
- Never bake distress into the master wordmark.
- Never reduce text contrast or product color accuracy.
- Never combine more than two texture techniques in one composition.

## 6. Imagery and art direction

### Campaign direction

- Chennai after dark, humid blue hour, modernist concrete, wet stone, warm sodium edge light.
- Real Indian creators moving before posing.
- Low 24-35 mm angles, documentary energy, strong negative space.
- Near-black, bone, deep indigo, and small acid detail.
- Product remains legible; dark-on-dark garments require edge separation.

### Product direction

Every hero product needs:

- Front, back, side, and three-quarter views.
- Consistent cutout scale on Paper.
- On-body front/back and movement.
- Fabric macro.
- Construction/technique macro.
- One climate/use frame.
- One three-way styling story.

### Image ratios

- Hero: 2.18:1 desktop master plus 4:5/9:16 mobile alternate crop.
- Product cutout: 4:5.
- Editorial card: 3:4 or 16:9.
- Story wide: 16:9 or 2:1.
- Customer fit: 4:5, moderated and rights-cleared.

### Image prohibitions

- No copied competitor photography or product silhouettes.
- No sacred/religious marks used as surface graphics without authored context and review.
- No generic neon cyberpunk, supercars, empty warehouses, or “luxury” clichés.
- No image-generation artifacts presented as real product proof.

## 7. Component system

### Foundation components

- Wordmark and K-device.
- Header, mega-menu, mobile drawer.
- Display heading, section heading, body, metadata, price.
- Button: primary, inverse, text, icon.
- Link: inline, navigation, underlined action.
- Input, search, checkbox, radio, select/listbox.
- Status chip: new, restock, sold out, limited, archive.
- Focus ring, divider, surface, scrim.

### Commerce components

- Product card and editorial-spanning card.
- Filter trigger/panel/chip/result count.
- Color swatch with text equivalent.
- Size selector with unavailable/restock states.
- Price and compare-at treatment.
- Product media gallery and video.
- Fit profile, measurement table, model record.
- Proof stack: fabric, fit, make, wear, service.
- Delivery/pincode module.
- Review summary, review card, customer-fit card.
- Complete-the-fit module.
- Cart line, quantity, cart drawer, cart summary.

### Editorial components

- Release hero.
- Wordmark bridge.
- Split story chapter.
- Full-bleed image chapter.
- Quote and creator credit.
- Product annotation.
- Story card and chapter index.
- Waitlist/restock module.

### Operational components

- Loading skeleton with fixed geometry.
- Empty/zero state.
- Inline error and error summary.
- Toast/live region.
- Consent preference panel.
- Help/fit concierge entry.
- Order status timeline.

## 8. Page blueprints

### Home

```text
[transparent header]
[cinematic CHAKSU release hero]
[cropped wordmark bridge]
[4-product release edit + one complete-fit span]
[fit/material/make proof strip]
[dark creator movement story]
[shop by intent]
[customer and creator proof]
[2-3 story cards]
[release-access signup]
[static utility footer]
```

Home should contain 8-10 meaningful sections maximum. The first product appears by approximately 1.5-2 viewport heights on desktop and sooner on mobile.

### Collection

- Bone/Paper surface by default.
- Small collection context, then commerce controls.
- 2/3/4-column product grid.
- One editorial card per 8-12 products maximum.
- Product card information remains visually stable.
- Load-more pagination, not footer-destroying infinite scroll.

### Product detail

- Desktop 7/5 split with sticky purchase rail.
- Mobile media first; purchase action immediately after essential selection.
- Product proof starts before long brand prose.
- Policy summary and delivery estimate appear above reviews.
- Complete-the-fit uses actual compatible variants and availability.

### Story

- Strong title/dek and creator credits.
- Chapter index only when more than four chapters.
- Alternating light/dark editorial scenes.
- Product annotations are quiet but unambiguous.
- End with archive/related release and owned-audience action.

### About

- What CHAKSU believes.
- Founder/team truth when available.
- Place and production context.
- Materials and making commitments.
- What the brand refuses to do.
- Direct path to current release and contact.

### Help

- Searchable questions.
- Delivery, returns, sizing, care, order tracking, contact.
- Effective dates and plain-language summaries.
- Avoid hiding critical exceptions in accordion-only fine print.

## 9. Responsive behavior

### Mobile first decisions

- Mobile gets the same art direction, not a reduced desktop screenshot.
- Use an art-directed 4:5/9:16 campaign crop; do not crop the subject by automation alone.
- Remove product-card hover dependencies.
- Replace complex parallax with static or shallow section movement.
- Filters use a full-height sheet; applied state remains visible.
- PDP purchase CTA becomes sticky only after the normal CTA exits view.
- Keep navigation, search, bag, and fit help reachable with one hand.

### Breakpoint behavior

| Pattern | Mobile | Tablet | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Header | compact 4-action | compact/expanded | full navigation |
| Mega-menu | drawer | drawer or mega | editorial mega-menu |
| Product grid | 2 columns | 3 columns | 4 columns |
| PDP | stacked/swipe | stacked or 6/6 | 7/5 sticky rail |
| Story split | stacked | 5/3 or 4/4 | 7/5 or 6/6 |
| Parallax | static/shallow | selected scenes | full progressive enhancement |

## 10. Interaction design

### Buttons

- Primary on light: Ink fill, Bone text.
- Primary on dark: Bone fill, Ink text.
- Campaign accent may use Acid only for a single prioritized action.
- Minimum 44 px height mobile and clear loading/disabled state.
- Loading preserves width and label context.

### Product card

- Whole image/title area may link; wishlist remains a separate control.
- Hover swaps to one alternate view, never a flashing slideshow.
- Focus state shows the same information as hover.
- Quick add opens a size selector; unavailable products offer restock.

### Menus and drawers

- 180-320 ms transition.
- Backdrop opacity no more than necessary.
- No blurred glass that reduces contrast or GPU performance.
- Body scroll lock with scrollbar compensation.

### Feedback

- Selection responds within 100 ms visually.
- Cart mutation shows optimistic state only if recovery is robust.
- Errors say what happened, what remains saved, and what to do.
- Success toasts do not steal focus and never contain the only next action.

## 11. State matrix

| Component | Default | Hover/focus | Loading | Empty/unavailable | Error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product card | Product/price | alt image + visible focus | fixed-ratio skeleton | sold-out/restock | fallback media |
| Variant | available | outline + label | disabled stable | crossed/announced | selection message |
| Add to bag | enabled after size | high-contrast focus | spinner + label | disabled with reason | inline retry |
| Filters | current count | highlighted trigger | preserve controls | clear recovery | previous results stay |
| Cart | line items | edit/remove focus | line-level pending | useful empty state | exact affected line |
| Story media | poster/image | play/focus | reserved ratio | credited fallback | omit nonessential block |

## 12. Accessibility design requirements

- WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Body 16-18 px; line height 1.45-1.65.
- Metadata never carries critical information alone.
- Visible 2 px focus ring with offset on all surfaces.
- Semantic source order matches reading order.
- Product swatches have text names; size states are announced.
- Minimum 44×44 px mobile target; 8 px separation where targets cluster.
- Reduced-motion comps are part of the design delivery, not a developer interpretation.
- Every video comp includes poster, pause, captions/transcript state.
- Error, empty, loading, sold-out, and long-text states are designed.

## 13. Design deliverables

1. Foundations: color, grid, spacing, typography, iconography, texture, motion.
2. Wordmark/K-device usage examples and protected areas.
3. Component library with all interaction and validation states.
4. Responsive Home, Collection, PDP, Story, Cart, Search, Help, Account/order views.
5. Reduced-motion variants for every animated composition.
6. Media crop and focal-point matrix.
7. Content length guidance and real launch copy; final handoff contains no dummy prose.
8. Prototype of navigation, hero handoff, filtering, PDP selection, cart, and checkout handoff.
9. Accessibility annotations and keyboard/focus map.
10. Developer specs: tokens, component anatomy, responsive rules, state behaviors, and asset exports.

## 14. Design review gates

- Five-second brand/proposition comprehension.
- Mobile product discovery and PDP usability with target customers.
- Contrast, focus, zoom, reduced-motion, and screen-reader structure review.
- Product color and crop approval by merchandising.
- Service/policy copy approved by operations.
- Performance review of every proposed hero, video, font, and animation.
- Originality review against the supplied reference and competitor material.
- No final UI uses licensed fonts before commercial webfont rights are confirmed.

## 15. Definition of award-worthy

“Award-winning” is not more animation. The website qualifies only if its idea is unmistakable and the system is complete:

- CHAKSU is recognisable without the logo through crop, K-device, palette, casting, and motion restraint.
- The first viewport creates a memorable emotional beat.
- Product proof is art-directed rather than appended.
- Mobile feels intentional and fast.
- Accessibility and reduced motion preserve the same quality.
- The story, product, service, and post-purchase experience express one brand—not four disconnected systems.
