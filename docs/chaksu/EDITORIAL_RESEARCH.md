# CHAKSU Editorial Research — Stories & About

## Purpose

This note records the evidence and editorial decisions behind the production copy for `/stories` and `/about`. It is a content-governance artifact, not customer-facing copy.

## Inputs reviewed

- CHAKSU `PRD.md`, `UI UX design Brief.md`, `Typography.md`, `web flow.md`, and `Implementation.md`.
- Supplied Urban Monkey and VYBN competitor-research prompts.
- Supplied Almost Gods design audit and CHAKSU typography direction.
- Official brand pages:
  - Almost Gods: <https://almostgods.com/pages/about-us>
  - Urban Monkey: <https://www.urbanmonkey.com/pages/about-us>
  - BLUORNG: <https://bluorng.com/pages/new-about-us>

## Research synthesis

1. **The category overuses abstract claims.** “Premium,” “culture,” “community,” “quality,” and “authenticity” recur across competitor narratives. CHAKSU should make those ideas observable instead of repeating the vocabulary.
2. **The strongest brand stories have a specific territory.** Almost Gods uses conceptual cultural systems; Urban Monkey uses an established skate/underground community history; BLUORNG uses founder and craft/quality positioning. CHAKSU cannot credibly borrow any of these foundations.
3. **CHAKSU's defensible territory is already defined.** Chennai as lived environment, movement as a design constraint, the K as a repeatable formal system, and proof before adjectives are coherent across the supplied strategy documents.
4. **Founder mythology is not yet available.** No founder names, founding date, workshop, manufacturing partner, awards, certifications, or verified material/performance records were supplied. The About page deliberately does not invent them.
5. **Editorial must hand off to commerce quietly.** Stories link to one relevant product each. They do not become disguised product grids.

## Customer-facing editorial architecture

### About

1. One-line identity and operating idea.
2. Chennai as design condition, not decoration.
3. Four operating principles.
4. Visual identity system: disciplined core plus controlled damage.
5. Truth standard for claims.
6. Links to Stories and the current release.

### Stories

1. Issue-based editorial hero.
2. One complete lead field note.
3. Three story entries across weather, identity, and utility.
4. Public editorial code covering people, process, and place.
5. Commercial handoff to the current release.

## Claims guardrail

Do not publish the following until the relevant owner supplies evidence and approval:

- Founder biography, founding year, or “origin story.”
- Fabric composition, GSM, waterproofing, durability, or performance claims.
- Factory, workshop, artisan, origin, or employment claims.
- Limited-edition quantities, scarcity, awards, press, celebrity placement, or sustainability claims.
- Creator/community participation without names, credits, consent, and usage rights.
